# x402 + Agentic.Market research — 2026-09-25

## Protocol

- HTTP-native payments using `402 Payment Required`. v2 headers: `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` / `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` / `PAYMENT-RESPONSE`.
- Neutral steward: Linux Foundation x402 Foundation (operational mid-2026). Coinbase CDP facilitator remains the highest-volume rail (USDC on Base and Solana).
- Public stats disagree on totals but agree on scale: tens of millions of txs / tens of millions USD, mostly **sub-dollar** calls. Organic seller count is small; headline volume includes loops and self-dealing.
- Schemes that matter for agents: `exact` and `upto`.
