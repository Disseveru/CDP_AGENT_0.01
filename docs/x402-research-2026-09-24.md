## What x402 is

x402 is an open HTTP-native payment protocol that activates the reserved `402 Payment Required` status. A client (human or agent) requests a resource; if unpaid, the server returns payment terms; the client retries with a signed payload; a facilitator verifies and settles, usually in USDC. No accounts, API keys, or checkout UI.

V2 headers: `PAYMENT-REQUIRED`, `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE`, `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` (base64 JSON). Schemes: `exact` (fixed amount) and `upto` (ceiling, capture actual usage). Coinbase created the spec; the **x402 Foundation under the Linux Foundation** now stewards it (operational 14 Jul 2026). Members include Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Adyen, Amex, AWS, Google, Cloudflare, Circle, Shopify, Ripple, Solana Foundation, Stellar, and dozens more.

## Scale (treat as upper bound)

Public dashboards disagree, but the order of magnitude is consistent:

- x402.org / Foundation comms: ~75M txs and ~$24M volume in a recent 30-day window; ~94k buyers / ~22k sellers.
- Agentic.market launch narrative (Apr 2026): 165M+ txs, ~$50M, hundreds of thousands of agents.
- Solana share of agent txs recently cited around 70–76% of some windows despite Base remaining the flagship CDP rail.
- Organic filter (x402stats, Sep 2026): only ~78 wallets cleared a real-commerce bar on $1.2M organic volume; median seller made cents; top 10 wallets ~79% of volume.
- TRM: most on-chain x402 flow is **not clearly agentic** (scripts, loops, self-dealing).

Implication: listing is cheap; **repeat inner-loop SKUs** make money. Protocol fee is ~0; you earn gross USDC at `payTo` minus gas (often facilitator-sponsored).

## Whitespace vs crowded

**Do not ship:** another CoinGecko/Firecrawl/OpenAI proxy, X/LinkedIn scrapers, physical checkout.

**Buy-again SKUs for agents (ranked):**

1. Receipt + SLA pack — proof a paid call settled and what was delivered.
2. Facilitator / rail health + **settle poll** (shipped 1.11.0 as `poll_facilitator_settle`).
3. Child-agent budget / `upto` session guards.
4. Seller-host dossier (first-seen, unique payers, 402 hygiene) — not another token-risk clone.
5. Sandboxed eval of untrusted seller JSON (no general compute).

Price band that loops can afford: **$0.002–$0.05** per call.
